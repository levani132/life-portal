# Food profile, activity targets and Apple Health

Approved scope: introduce per-step onboarding completion without resetting module choices;
collect separate everyday movement and exercise inputs; retain editable known profile data;
connect via a shared read-only Apple Shortcut and scoped revocable upload tickets; adjust
calorie targets from active energy for matching periods without counting workout/step energy
twice; preserve uncertain forecasts; show four nutrition rings and a distinct activity value;
keep the homepage wall and redirect old Wall routes.

Acceptance: no-data targets stable; duplicates and overlapping sources not summed; custom day
boundary and timezone honored; missing/zero activity does not prove inactivity; authenticated
uploads cannot select another owner; last-sync set only after validated receipt. Device-only
Shortcut signing, publication, permissions, handoff and browser confirmations remain explicit.
